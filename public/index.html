Demo story

What this prototype proves

1. Capture incomplete data A user can describe an unclear investment or paste document text.
2. Draft, do not decide The assistant classifies, scores confidence, and highlights missing fields.
3. Confirm into ledger Only reviewed items affect the confirmed net worth calculation.
4. Indicate goal levers The planner shows scenario math, not financial advice.
